How Margadarshi works

Margadarshi means guide. You enter your current education, interests, passion and skills. A large language model (Google Gemini) reads them and writes a route map made just for you. There is no database of careers, so a different profile gives a different map.

What you get

Career options, courses, entrance exams, skills to build, job roles, risks and trade-offs, next steps, and a flowchart of the whole journey with decision points.

Good to know

The map is a starting guide, not a promise of admission, income or a job. Exams, dates and eligibility change, so check official websites and talk to teachers or a counsellor before you decide.
